After operational recovery, the competing scientific explanations remain:

| Explanation | Observation that would distinguish it |
|---|---|
| Omitted objective contribution | Independently evaluated slack/input/stage contribution closes the signed gap at the same iterate. |
| Formulas equivalent only on feasible solutions | Alternatives separate when the relevant equality constraint is violated, with differences matching that violation. |
| Vector, parameter, or scaling mismatch | Direct NLP evaluation and reconstruction use demonstrably different coordinates, or direct evaluation disagrees with the recorded solver objective. |
