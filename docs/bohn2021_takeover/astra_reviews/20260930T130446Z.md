**Severity-ranked findings**

1. **High — T-B6 currently conflates two logically independent hypotheses.**  
   Stable IDs: `A13_objective_reconstruction_contract_localization`, `A11_training_failure_modes_need_separation`.

   The archived author [vehicle controller](/data/openai-agent/mobile-robot-mppi-study/research_artifacts/bohn2021_reproduction_2026-09-17/sources/gym-horizon/gym_let_mpc/controllers.py:831) creates both hard and soft obstacle constraints, with soft penalty **1000**. For obstacle center distance \(d\) and radius \(r\), these correspond to

   \[
   d\ge r,\qquad 1.5r-d\le\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\ge0.
   \]

   Thus, a trajectory with \(r\le d<1.5r\) can satisfy the hard constraint and the softened NLP constraints while having **positive optimal slack**. The [author optimizer](/data/openai-agent/mobile-robot-mppi-study/research_artifacts/bohn2021_reproduction_2026-09-17/sources/do-mpc-horizon/do_mpc/optimizer.py:298) explicitly documents this semantics and constructs `slack_cost += penalty_term_cons*epsilon` at line 336. The [author objective assembly](/data/openai-agent/mobile-robot-mppi-study/research_artifacts/bohn2021_reproduction_2026-09-17/sources/do-mpc-horizon/do_mpc/controller.py:1090) adds the discounted `epsterm` at every stage.

   Therefore:

   - The omitted term is a **verified reconstruction-formula defect relative to this source-documented objective**.
   - `Solve_Succeeded` does **not** imply `epsterm_total≈0`.
   - A converged cell with correct four-component reconstruction but inaccurate three-component reconstruction would falsify **H2**, the zero-slack-at-convergence hypothesis. It would **not** falsify **H-MAIN**, the omitted-slack explanation.

   The [active plan’s failure branch](/data/openai-agent/mobile-robot-mppi-study/docs/bohn2021_takeover/opus_lead/20260930T125337Z_10774a.md:85), particularly line 92, currently makes that invalid implication.
