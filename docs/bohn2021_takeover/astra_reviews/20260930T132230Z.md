   Stable IDs: `A11_training_failure_modes_need_separation`, `A6_strong_fixed_H_and_terminal_opportunity_not_closed`.

   \[
   d\ge r,\qquad 1.5r-d\le\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\ge0,
   \]

   with penalty coefficient **1000**. A converged solution can satisfy the hard and softened constraints while using positive slack inside the soft margin. The [optimizer](/data/openai-agent/mobile-robot-mppi-study/research_artifacts/bohn2021_reproduction_2026-09-17/sources/do-mpc-horizon/do_mpc/optimizer.py:298) explicitly documents this behavior; the [objective assembly](/data/openai-agent/mobile-robot-mppi-study/research_artifacts/bohn2021_reproduction_2026-09-17/sources/do-mpc-horizon/do_mpc/controller.py:1090) already includes discounted slack cost.

   For the inspected single-scenario vehicle configuration,

   \[
   J_\epsilon=\sum_{k=0}^{H-1}\gamma^k\sum_j1000\,\epsilon_{k,j}.

   Therefore, positive slack at convergence would falsify the universal zero-slack interpretation of H2. It would not invalidate the omitted-slack diagnosis.

No evidence here shows that adding it to the reconstruction changes deployed actions. The broader true-variable-H work remains IMPROVED, but the slack term itself is author-source behavior.
