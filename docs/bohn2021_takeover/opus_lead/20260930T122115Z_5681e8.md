## 4. Root-cause framing (the part that matters beyond this one file)

The recurring failure mode is structural, not clerical: **a rotating artifact is pinned as a gate constant.** `PLAN_READY.json` is rewritten by the supervisor every cycle and by the active lead every report; `LATEST.md` likewise. Any wrapper that hard-codes their values becomes stale exactly when the lead publishes a new plan — which is precisely when the executor is authorised to launch. Seven consecutive zero-solve cycles have now produced no solver number for U2, and at least two of them were consumed by this single class.

Discriminating fix: the gate must verify **internal consistency of the live plan**, not equality with a frozen copy. Specifically, the wrapper should demand that (i) the file named in `PLAN_READY.json.report` hashes to `PLAN_READY.json.report_sha256`, (ii) that same report path appears in `LATEST.md`, and (iii) `PLAN_READY.json.request_id` equals the provenance request under which the executor is running. Expected request/sha should be *derived*, not asserted against a literal. That preserves the integrity property (you cannot run v34t under a plan that did not authorise v34t) while eliminating the staleness race.
