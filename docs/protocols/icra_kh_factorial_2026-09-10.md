# Small fixed K x H identification experiment

This returns to the original budget-adaptation question. It does not test an online joint policy and does not reuse fixed-surrogate results as joint-adaptation evidence.

Forty-two serial development episodes: two prequalified physical corridors(single_turn/reverse_turns), original direction, standard mass/inertia, one residual model block0 and matching trained RL geometry policy block0, three fresh evaluation seeds12620001-3, seven methods. Four factorial treatments are full ICODE K32/K100 x H16/H36. Strong controls: full ICODE K64/H36, nominal K100/H28, matched Bohn-inspired RL-H K100/H8-50 with its existing terminal-value augmentation. No surrogate approximation, extra terminal value, or new training in the four factorial treatments. All common plant/safety/noise/Euler/speed0.35/120s settings preserved. H16=1.6s and H36=3.6s at dt0.1. Different K consumes different random streams, so seed matching is a blocked comparison, not identical candidate banks.

Randomized method order within shape/seed blocks; all seven methods first run the common single-turn seed12620001. If any fails, retain results and stop expansion for controller qualification. Otherwise complete all42 without interim accuracy selection. Freeze sources/protocol/checkpoints. Record full traces/configs, actual H, configured K, success/collision, RMSE and measured compute. No concurrent training/heavy analysis.

Analysis: all14method/shape cell means, three paired seeds each; report factorial effects at each level, and per-seed difference-of-differences in RMSE and compute. A crossover or nonzero empirical interaction only motivates further study; it does not prove statistical significance, causal observability, benefit of switching within an episode, or a unique best budget absent a specified objective. A simpler fixed setting winning broadly is an explicit alternative. No significance claims from three seeds, no simulator-mass/shape label oracle treated as an online algorithm. The current factorial is development evidence; any selected scheduler requires independent confirmation and K-only/H-only controls.
